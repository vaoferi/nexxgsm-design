## SEO Strategy

### Indian Version (en-IN)
- **Hreflang**: `en-IN`
- **Schema**: LocalBusiness + Service (near me intent)
- **Content**: Blog posts + service pages + city-specific landing pages
- **Backlinks**: Local directories (Justdial, Sulekha), tech forums (Digit, 91mobiles)
- **Meta**: Include "India", city names, pricing in INR

### American Version (en-US)
- **Hreflang**: `en-US`
- **Schema**: Organization + Service + TechArticle
- **Content**: Knowledge base + enterprise solutions + model-specific guides
- **Backlinks**: Tech publications (Tom's Hardware, PCMag), enterprise forums
- **Meta**: Model numbers, warranty mentions, USD pricing
